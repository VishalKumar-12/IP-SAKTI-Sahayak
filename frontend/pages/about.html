About

Why IP-SAKTI Sahayak exists

An overview of the problem, the technology, and the safety principles behind the assistant.

01

What is IP-SAKTI?

IP-SAKTI Sahayak is an AI-powered assistant that helps Ayurveda practitioners, researchers, startups, students and innovators understand intellectual property and regulatory questions — patents, trademarks, copyright, geographical indications, biodiversity, Access and Benefit-Sharing, traditional knowledge, TKDL-related information, product classification, AYUSH regulations, and international IPR frameworks.

02

Why this problem matters

Ayurveda innovation sits at the intersection of modern IPR law, traditional knowledge protection and biodiversity regulation. Many innovators struggle to find clear, trustworthy guidance across these overlapping frameworks — leading to missed protections, inadvertent non-compliance, or reliance on informal advice.

03

How RAG works

IP-SAKTI uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): rather than answering purely from a language model's internal memory, it first retrieves relevant passages from a curated knowledge base of acts, treaties and official documents, then generates an answer grounded strictly in that retrieved evidence.

  • Documents are chunked and embedded with rich metadata (jurisdiction, category, section, page, year).
  • Hybrid retrieval combines semantic (vector) search with keyword search.
  • A reranking step surfaces only the most relevant evidence before generation.
04

Why citations matter

Every meaningful legal or regulatory claim is paired with a citation to its source document, section and page. IP-SAKTI never invents sections, articles, laws, treaties, cases or citations — if a citation cannot be verified against the retrieved evidence, it is not shown as verified.

05

How jurisdiction detection works

Questions are analyzed to determine the most relevant jurisdiction — India or International by default, with USA, UK, EU and Japan planned for the future. If authoritative information for a selected jurisdiction is not yet available in the knowledge base, IP-SAKTI says so directly rather than guessing.

06

How the ABS Helper works

The ABS Helper analyzes a described biological resource, its type, intended purpose and jurisdiction to flag potential Access and Benefit-Sharing considerations under frameworks such as the Biological Diversity Act, the CBD and the Nagoya Protocol — presented as indicators for further review, not final legal determinations.

07

How the TKDL Helper works

The TKDL Helper surfaces possible traditional-knowledge and prior-art indicators for a herb, formulation or treatment using authorized and publicly available information only. It never scrapes, exposes or reproduces restricted TKDL database contents, and directs users to official TKDL resources for authoritative searches.

08

Safety and disclaimer

IP-SAKTI is built with guardrails that prevent it from fabricating legal facts. When the knowledge base lacks sufficient evidence, it abstains rather than guesses, and recommends professional verification. All output includes a confidence score and a clear disclaimer.
